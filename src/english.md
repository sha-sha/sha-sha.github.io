I provide therapy for individuals, couples and families. I specialize in Emotionally Focused Therapy, where I help my clients cope better with the challenges they are facing. Recent scientific research in Neurobilology has provided us with extensive knowledge about the biology of our emotions. We know more about the physiology of our feelings and what can we do to alleviate the effects of stress and distress on our well-being. In therapy I utilize this knowledge to help my clients understand themselves better, to gain better control over their reactions such as anger or withdrawal, and to gradually improve their responses to stressors in their relationships, at work, etc. I integrate mindfulness methods to provide my clients with tools for coping more effectively with stress, anxiety, anger and distress.

 
# About Myself

I received my M.Sc and Ph.D at the Weizmann Institute of Science studying Developmental Biology.
I then studied Clinical Psychology at AULA in California, and did my internship at SCCC, where I was fortunate to train with leading therapists in the US, including Estelle Fisher, Jonathan Flier, Robert Mendelsohn and Tzvia Amber. I trained and practiced in the most advanced practices in psychology, including Narrative psychology, Interpersonal Gestalt Therapy, and Trauma and Resilience Focused Therapy.

**I am fluent in Hebrew and English**
